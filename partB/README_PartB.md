# partB: ray-traced channels and no-RIS baseline

Tool: NVIDIA Sionna RT 2.2.0 (Mitsuba 3, CPU). Carrier 28 GHz, bandwidth 100 MHz, BS 40 dBm EIRP (isotropic), noise −87 dBm.
Ray tracing: max depth 4, LOS + specular reflection + edge diffraction, no transmission through buildings, 10⁶ rays per source (Sionna RT default), seed 42.

Scene (`../scene/`): synthetic 220 m × 160 m intersection, main street along x, side street along y, four buildings 18–25 m tall (concrete, brick, glass), a metal kiosk in the side street, a metal truck in the main street, concrete ground. BS at (−20, 6, 10) m. UE grid: side street, 1 m spacing, 1.5 m height, 19 × 85 = 1,615 points, of which the 1,587 outside the kiosk footprint are used in the paper. RIS candidates R1–R6 are on facades at 6 m height.

Generated by `export_partB.py` (CSV and JSON files) and `fig_partB.py` (figure), from `../out/rt_paths.npz`.

## Files
| File | Content |
|---|---|
| channel_paths_BS_to_UE.csv | direct channel h_d: the strongest 16 ray-traced paths per UE |
| channel_paths_BS_to_RIS.csv | BS → centre of each RIS candidate |
| channel_paths_RIS_to_UE.csv | centre of each RIS candidate → each UE |
| RIS_candidates_BS_link.csv | each RIS candidate: position, LOS to the BS, ray-traced and free-space BS→RIS gain |
| noRIS_baseline_per_UE.csv | per grid point: valid flag, LOS flag, number of paths, path gain, received power, SNR (coherent and local mean), complex h_d, coverage flags |
| noRIS_SNR_by_distance.csv | mean SNR and coverage in 10 m bands along the side street (valid UEs) |
| noRIS_radiomap_full_area_1m.csv | radio map of the whole scene at 1 m resolution (local mean), from Sionna RadioMapSolver |
| noRIS_summary.json | headline no-RIS numbers (valid UEs) |
| fig_noRIS_sidestreet.png | Figure 3 of the paper: SNR maps over the side street and SNR versus distance |

Channel-path columns: tx, rx, path_rank (1 = strongest), gain_dB, phase_rad, amp_real and amp_imag (linear complex amplitude, scientific notation), length_m, delay_ns, n_interactions (0 = LOS), type, and the first and last interaction points (first_vertex_x/y/z, last_vertex_x/y/z).

## Notes
- `noRIS_baseline_per_UE.csv` has all 1,615 grid points; the 1,587 used in the paper have `valid = True`. The channel files list every grid point that received at least one path, including some inside the kiosk; join on the UE ID and keep `valid = True` rows to match the paper.
- Three valid UEs (UE0920–UE0922, behind the kiosk) receive no path without an RIS. They are stored at −173 dB SNR and, like every UE, counted at the −60 dB floor in mean-SNR figures.

## Two SNR definitions
- `SNR_dB` (primary): |sum of complex path amplitudes|², the narrowband channel of Eq. (1), including multipath fading.
- `SNR_localmean_dB`: sum of |path amplitude|², the average power over a small area (no fading), as in Sionna radio maps.

## Per-element RIS channels
The analysis scripts expand the RIS-centre paths to every element: for path k with amplitude a_k and interaction point v_k next to the RIS, element n gets a_k · exp(−j k₀ (|v_k − p_n| − |v_k − c|)) · sqrt(G cos θ_k), with G = π for λ/2 elements (Eq. 4 of the paper). The v_k are the `last_vertex_*` columns for BS→RIS paths and the `first_vertex_*` columns for RIS→UE paths.
